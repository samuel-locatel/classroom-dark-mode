features

Six small decisions, made once.

IOne toggle

One toggle

Turn it on, turn it off, forget about it. No themes, no profiles, no setup wizard — one toolbar click and you're back to studying.

IIReading-first type

Reading-first type

Choose between serif, sans, or monospace for body copy — Classroom Dark Mode recomposes the page without breaking Classroom's layout.

IIIFollows the sun

Follows the sun

Light mode during the day, dark at night — using your local time. Or lock in one mode if you'd rather.

IVDimmed media

Dimmed media

Attachments and video covers get a soft veil so the page doesn't flicker as you scroll. Original colors return on hover.

VNo telemetry

No telemetry

Classroom Dark Mode sends no data to any server. All your preferences stay in your browser. Open source, auditable.

VI14 kB · zero deps

14 kB · zero deps

A single carefully-injected stylesheet. Works offline, loads before Classroom's content, doesn't delay your login.
